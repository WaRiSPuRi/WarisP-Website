About

Dedicated student with a well-rounded background in STEM, business, and community leadership. As a member of the St. Kateri Robotics Team, the Lead Financial Officer for STEAM ICAC, and a DECA Provincials qualifier, I have built a strong foundation in technical problem-solving and strategic business communication.

I also run my own small business, Waris Services, offering lawn care, car washing, and pressure washing to neighbours in the Cobban neighbourhood of Milton. What started as helping out neighbours has turned into a business I'm proud of, with five-star reviews from repeat customers.

My experience as a Peer Mentor, Army Cadet, and participant in the Halton Police Peace Program shows my commitment to teamwork and leadership. I'm First Aid/CPR certified and trained in HIGH FIVE® principles, with hands-on experience as a Swimming Instructor and Kumon volunteer. I also take photos at school and cadet events, and I speak four languages.
